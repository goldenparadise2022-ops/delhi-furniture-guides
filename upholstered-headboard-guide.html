Upholstered Headboard Guide: Height, Padding and Fabric for Indian Bedrooms

An upholstered headboard makes reading in bed more comfortable and softens a bedroom's look. In Delhi, dust, humidity and hair oil all affect how a fabric headboard ages, so height, padding and fabric deserve as much attention as colour. Use these figures as starting points and adjust for your bed and wall.

Before you buy

  • Sit up in bed against a wall and measure where your head and shoulders rest.
  • Check switchboard and window positions behind the bed.
  • Ask if the panel is removable for cleaning or re-covering.
  • Leave a small gap from damp external walls during the monsoon.
  • Choose darker or textured fabric where hair oil is a daily habit.

Common mistakes

  • Covering the only switchboard beside the bed.
  • Choosing a headboard so tall it clashes with a low window sill.
  • Picking very pale velvet in a room that gets road dust.
