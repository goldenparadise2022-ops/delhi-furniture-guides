Marble Dining Table Care Guide

Simple habits protect marble from stains, etching and heat marks in busy Indian homes.

IssuePreventionWhat to do
Tea or curry spillUse coasters and placematsBlot, then wipe with pH-neutral cleaner
Hot vesselUse trivetsNever place directly on stone
ScratchesAvoid dragging crockeryUse felt pads and a soft cloth
EtchingKeep lemon and vinegar off surfaceAsk a stone professional to refinish

Weekly checklist

  • Dust with a dry microfiber cloth.
  • Wipe spills promptly and dry the surface.
  • Inspect joints, edge chips and table stability.
  • Refresh sealant only on the stone-care professional's advice.
