Wardrobe Planning for a 2BHK Delhi Flat

A practical guide to sizing wardrobes for bedrooms in compact Delhi apartments.

BedroomSuggested widthDepthNotes
Primary1800–2400 mm600 mmFull-height shutters and loft

Measurement checklist

  • Measure wall, skirting and ceiling height.
  • Keep 750 mm minimum bed-to-wardrobe passage.
  • Check window, switchboard and door swing.
  • Plan hanging, folded-clothes and luggage zones.
