Will Your Furniture Fit Through the Door?

Delivery planning is part of furniture buying. Measure the route from the building entrance to the room, not only the room itself.

Measure the route

  • Building gate and security turn
  • Entrance and apartment door clear opening
  • Lift interior, lift door, and diagonal clearance
  • Corridor width, ceiling height, and stair landings
  • Sharp turns, railings, and low balconies

Make a delivery plan

Ask for packaged and assembled dimensions. Protect walls and floors, reserve the lift if required by your society, and remove fragile décor. Tell the delivery team about parking or access restrictions in advance. If a piece is modular, confirm how it will be assembled inside the room.

Final checklist

Keep the tape-measure notes and photos ready, confirm the delivery appointment, and inspect corners, finish, drawers, and hardware before signing.
