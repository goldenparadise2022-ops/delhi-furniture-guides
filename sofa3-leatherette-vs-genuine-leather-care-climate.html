Leatherette vs Genuine Leather 3-Seater Sofas: Care and Climate Comparison

Leather-look three-seaters are popular for easy wiping, but genuine leather and leatherette (PU or PVC) behave differently in Delhi heat and humidity. This comparison focuses on everyday care rather than prestige. Keep a pen handy: the table and checklist are meant to be filled in and compared, not just read.

Before you buy

  • Ask whether the upholstery is genuine leather, bonded leather or PU.
  • Check what the care label recommends.
  • Keep the sofa away from direct heaters and sun.
  • Test comfort in summer clothing.

How to apply this

Know the material

Terms such as bonded leather, top grain and PU are not interchangeable. Ask the seller to specify the material on the invoice so the care instructions match.

Heat sources

Room heaters placed close to leather or leatherette in winter can dry or damage the surface. Keep a safe distance and follow the manufacturer's guidance.

A note on adapting this

Treat the suggestions here as a starting framework rather than fixed rules. Fabrics, finishes and household routines differ, so the care label and the seller's instructions always take priority over general advice.

A home with young children may value leatherette's easy wipe, while a household that wants natural ageing may prefer genuine leather with a care routine.

Every household is different, so adapt these suggestions rather than following them rigidly. What suits a couple in a studio may not suit a joint family, and the person who uses the sofa most should have the loudest say. Share the notes with everyone at home before ordering, since a second opinion often catches something missed.

Pitfalls worth noting

  • Picking a colour under showroom lighting only.
  • Forgetting how pets, children or dust will interact with the surface.
  • Overlooking how the material feels on bare arms in summer.

A simple sequence

Ask the seller what the material is, how it should be cleaned and what the warranty covers, and keep the answers with your order confirmation. Store the care label photo, invoice and your notes together; they will be useful long after delivery day.

Final recommendation

Whatever you choose, keep the care instructions with the invoice and follow them rather than general internet remedies.
