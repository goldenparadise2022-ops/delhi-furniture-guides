3-Seater Sofa for a 1BHK: Multi-Use Seating Planner

In a 1BHK, the living room often doubles as dining area, workspace and guest room. A three-seater there must earn its footprint, and this planner lists common uses and what each asks of the sofa. The notes are written with Delhi and NCR homes in mind, though the principles apply to most Indian households.

Checklist

  • List every use the sofa will have.
  • Rank the uses by frequency.
  • Choose a moderate width to keep circulation.
  • Pair with movable, multi-use tables.

What the table does not show

Prioritise daily uses

If the sofa is used for work every weekday and guests stay twice a year, prioritise upright support over sleeping length.

Movable companions

Nesting tables and pouffes adapt the room without adding permanent furniture.

Using the figures sensibly

Numbers are most useful when you test them. Mark outlines with painter's tape, sit on similar furniture you already own and write down what feels right, so you compare real sofas against your own notes instead of memory.

A single professional in a Saket 1BHK might choose a 1830 mm sofa with firm cushions and a C-shaped laptop table.

Treat every figure here as a planning target rather than a promise. Cushion softness, arm shape, rug thickness and household habits all change how a sofa feels and fits, so measure your own room and furniture and leave a little tolerance. Keep your measurements, swatch notes and photographs in one folder on your phone so the decision stays easy to check later.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Using overall dimensions when the seat dimensions matter more.
  • Placing the sofa first and squeezing everything else around it.
  • Leaving no space for a side table, lamp or charging point.

Suggested order of work

Photograph the room from the doorway, mark the planned sofa in tape and photograph it again. Seeing the before and after side by side often makes the right size obvious. A short written record of what you chose and why makes future cleaning, repairs or replacements much simpler.

Before you order

Write down a normal weekday and a normal weekend in the room; the sofa should suit both.
