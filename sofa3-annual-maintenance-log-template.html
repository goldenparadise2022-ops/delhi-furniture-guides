Practical checks

  • Keep the log with the invoice.
  • Note professional cleaning dates and vendors.
  • Record any repairs or replacement parts.
  • Photograph the sofa's condition once a year.

Making the numbers work at home

Why keep a log

A log shows when tasks were last done and helps you notice changes early, such as a leg loosening or a cushion flattening faster than the others.

Warranty records

Warranty questions are easier to raise with purchase dates, care records and photographs in one place.

Delhi home context

Delhi's seasons put different demands on furniture: dry, dusty heat, humid monsoon weeks and cool winters with heaters. Revisit your routine as the seasons change rather than setting it once.

A family might keep the log as a laminated card inside the TV unit and fill it in with a marker after each task.

When information is missing, ask. Sellers, manufacturers and professional cleaners can usually clarify materials, construction and care, and a written answer is worth more than a guess. Store the care label photo, invoice and your notes together; they will be useful long after delivery day.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Rubbing a spill instead of blotting it.
  • Using a cleaner without checking the care label first.
  • Waiting for a big annual clean instead of doing small regular tasks.

Suggested order of work

Keep a small kit of white cloths, a soft brush and the vacuum's upholstery head in one place, so the right tools are to hand when something happens. Keep your measurements, swatch notes and photographs in one folder on your phone so the decision stays easy to check later.
