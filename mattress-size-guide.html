Mattress Size and Firmness Guide for India

A mattress should suit your sleeping position, body weight, room size, and Delhi climate. This India-focused guide gives a practical starting point; test comfort in person when possible.

Common sizes and who they suit

SizeTypical usePlanning note
Single (72×36 in)One adult or childLeave walking clearance on one long side
Double (72×48 in)One adult with room to stretchCheck bedroom door and wardrobe clearance
Queen (78×60 in)Two adults in most bedroomsAllow at least 60 cm circulation where possible
King (78×72 in)Two adults wanting extra widthMeasure lift, staircase, and bedside space

Firmness checklist

  • Side sleepers usually prefer medium or medium-soft cushioning at the shoulder and hip.
  • Back sleepers often like medium-firm support that keeps the spine level.
  • Stomach sleepers generally need firmer, stable support.
  • Heavier sleepers may need a stronger support core; ask about weight limits.
  • For hot rooms, compare breathable covers and avoid trapping moisture.
  • Confirm trial, warranty, rotation, and cleaning instructions before purchase.
