Sofa Size Chart for Indian Apartments

Choosing a sofa is easier when you compare the overall width, depth, and clearance—not just the number of seats. Keep at least 75 cm for a comfortable walkway and check lift and door measurements.

TypeTypical widthBest for
Compact 2-seater120–160 cmStudio or narrow living room
Standard 3-seater180–220 cmMost apartment living rooms
L-shaped sectional240–300 cmOpen layouts with a clear corner

Before ordering

  1. Measure wall width and mark the sofa footprint with masking tape.
  2. Leave 45–60 cm between a sofa and coffee table.
  3. Measure the narrowest doorway, lift door, corridor, and stair landing.
  4. Confirm whether legs or cushions are removable for carrying.
