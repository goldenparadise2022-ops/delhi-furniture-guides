3-Seater Sofa and Split AC Airflow: A Placement Grid

Split ACs are common in Delhi living rooms. A three-seater placed directly under or in front of the indoor unit can mean cold draughts, condensation drips or blocked airflow. Everything below is practical guidance to adapt, not a set of promises about any particular product.

Planning grid

Checklist

  • Note where the indoor unit is mounted.
  • Trace the drain pipe route.
  • Sit in the sofa spot with the AC on.
  • Adjust louvres to avoid direct draughts.

What the table does not show

Draughts

Cold air aimed directly at seated heads can be uncomfortable. Offsetting the sofa or adjusting the louvres usually helps.

Condensation

Check that the AC drain pipe does not run behind or above the sofa, where a leak could reach the fabric.

Delhi home context

Delhi homes vary widely, from compact DDA flats and builder floors to large NCR apartments. Measure the clear floor rather than trusting brochure areas, and note doors, switchboards, windows and AC units before you shortlist anything.

A family might place the sofa on the wall opposite the AC so cool air travels across the room before reaching the seats.

Room shape, light and the way people move through the space matter as much as any single dimension. Sketch the room roughly to scale and pencil in the sofa, tables and walking routes before making a decision. Keep your measurements, swatch notes and photographs in one folder on your phone so the decision stays easy to check later.

What to avoid

  • Using overall dimensions when the seat dimensions matter more.
  • Placing the sofa first and squeezing everything else around it.
  • Leaving no space for a side table, lamp or charging point.

How to proceed

Photograph the room from the doorway, mark the planned sofa in tape and photograph it again. Seeing the before and after side by side often makes the right size obvious. A short written record of what you chose and why makes future cleaning, repairs or replacements much simpler.
