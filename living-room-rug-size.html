Living Room Rug Size Guide for Indian Apartments

A rug should connect seating without becoming a trip hazard. Tape the proposed footprint on the floor before buying.

Rug planning table

Seating layoutUseful rug sizePlacement rule
Compact 2-seater120 × 180 cmFront legs on rug or rug centred beneath table
3-seater with chairs160 × 230 cmInclude front legs of every seat
L-shape sofa200 × 300 cmRun the long side parallel to the sofa
Open living room240 × 340 cmKeep a consistent border from walls

Checklist for Delhi homes

  • Leave 30–45 cm around a coffee table for comfortable circulation.
  • Check that the rug does not cover floor sockets or block balcony doors.
  • Choose a low pile for dusty rooms and a non-slip underlay on smooth tile.
  • Confirm washable care instructions before monsoon season.
  • Measure the lift and stair route if ordering a thick rolled rug.
