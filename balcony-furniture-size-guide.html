Balcony Furniture Size Guide for Delhi Apartments

Delhi balconies are often narrow, shared with a washing machine or clothes-drying stand and used most in winter mornings and monsoon evenings. The trick is choosing furniture that leaves a walking path and does not block the drain or the sliding door. Measure depth first: it limits you more than length.

Size by balcony depth

Planning checklist

  • Leave a clear path of about 60 cm to the drying stand, plants or washing machine.
  • Keep the floor drain and slope clear so rainwater escapes.
  • Check the railing height stays safe: furniture should not help children climb.
  • Choose folding or stackable pieces if the balcony doubles as a utility area.
  • Confirm society rules on hanging swings or fixing to walls.

Common mistakes

  • Buying a showroom set without measuring the sliding-door track.
  • Placing chairs where the AC outdoor unit blows hot air.
  • Covering the drain with a large rug.

A couple in a Vasundhara flat with a 1.2 m deep balcony, for example, might pick a 65 cm round bistro table with two folding chairs that hang on a wall hook when clothes need drying space.
