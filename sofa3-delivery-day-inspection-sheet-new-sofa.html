Delivery Day Inspection Sheet for a New 3-Seater Sofa

Delivery day is the best time to spot issues, while the delivery team is still present. This sheet keeps the inspection quick and thorough. Keep a pen handy: the table and checklist are meant to be filled in and compared, not just read.

Before you buy

  • Clear the path before the team arrives.
  • Unpack in good light.
  • Photograph any issue before signing.
  • Keep the delivery note and invoice together.

How to apply this

Sign after checking

Where possible, inspect before signing the delivery note, and write any issues on it.

Level floors

Sit on each seat. A slight rock can be a floor issue, so try the sofa in another spot before assuming a defect.

Using the guidance sensibly

The most useful habits are the small ones repeated regularly. A few minutes each week usually does more for a sofa than an occasional marathon effort or a rushed last-minute decision.

A family receiving a sofa on a weekday might ask for a daytime slot so the inspection happens in natural light.

Treat the suggestions here as a starting framework rather than fixed rules. Fabrics, finishes and household routines differ, so the care label and the seller's instructions always take priority over general advice. Share the notes with everyone at home before ordering, since a second opinion often catches something missed.

Pitfalls worth noting

  • Confirming the order before checking the delivery route.
  • Relying on verbal answers instead of written confirmation.
  • Leaving delivery charges and floor access to the last minute.

A simple sequence

Gather dimensions, route photos and policy details in one note, check each listing against it, and keep screenshots of whatever you finally order. Store the care label photo, invoice and your notes together; they will be useful long after delivery day.

Final recommendation

Print the sheet or keep it on your phone, and tick each line while the team is still there.
