Solid Wood vs Veneer: Which Is Better?

Both materials can make beautiful, durable furniture. The right choice depends on the room, budget, design, and maintenance you prefer.

Solid wood

Solid timber has natural grain and can often be repaired or refinished. It is a good fit for dining tables and heirloom pieces, but it can be heavier and may move slightly with humidity. Use coasters and wipe spills promptly.

Wood veneer

Veneer is a thin layer of real wood over a stable engineered core. It offers consistent panels, lighter handling, and efficient use of timber. Choose a quality edge band and protect the surface from standing water and harsh cleaners.

Smart buying questions

  • Which surfaces are solid, veneered, or engineered?
  • What finish is used, and how should it be cleaned?
  • Are joints reinforced and hardware replaceable?
  • Is there a written warranty and care guidance?
