Guest Hosting Worksheet: When a 3-Seater Sofa Needs to Seat More

Festivals, kitty parties and cricket matches often bring more people than a three-seater comfortably holds. This worksheet plans the extra seating in advance. Keep a pen handy: the table and checklist are meant to be filled in and compared, not just read.

Household checklist

  • Count your typical festival guests.
  • Store floor cushions or pouffes where you can reach them.
  • Keep a path clear for serving.
  • Protect the sofa with a washable throw.

Details worth getting right

Floor seating tradition

Many Indian homes already use durries and floor cushions for larger gatherings. Planning storage for them keeps hosting easy.

Dining chairs as backup

Lightweight dining chairs can move to the living room. Leave space for them in the layout.

A note on measuring

Treat every figure here as a planning target rather than a promise. Cushion softness, arm shape, rug thickness and household habits all change how a sofa feels and fits, so measure your own room and furniture and leave a little tolerance.

A family hosting a Holi brunch might plan three on the sofa, four on dining chairs and children on floor cushions.

Every household is different, so adapt these suggestions rather than following them rigidly. What suits a couple in a studio may not suit a joint family, and the person who uses the sofa most should have the loudest say. A short written record of what you chose and why makes future cleaning, repairs or replacements much simpler.

Pitfalls worth noting

  • Planning the sofa in isolation from the centre table and TV unit.
  • Ignoring the route people take from the entrance to the kitchen.
  • Assuming showroom proportions will look the same in a smaller room.

A simple sequence

Decide what the room is mainly for, list the furniture it must hold, and give the sofa its place last. This order stops the sofa from quietly taking space that a walkway or side table needs. Write your final numbers on a single page and carry it when you visit showrooms or compare listings online.
