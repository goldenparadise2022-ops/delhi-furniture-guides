Small Living Room Layouts That Feel Open

A small room can work beautifully when circulation stays clear and every item earns its footprint.

Five practical ideas

  1. Choose a compact sofa: keep depth modest and use a slim arm profile.
  2. Float the seating: leave a small breathing space behind the sofa if it improves the walkway.
  3. Use nesting tables: they provide surface area without a permanent large block.
  4. Go vertical: a tall, shallow cabinet uses wall height while keeping floor space.
  5. Define zones: use a rug and lighting to separate seating from dining or work.

Spacing rule of thumb

Keep roughly 45–60 cm between seating and a coffee table, and aim for 75 cm or more for the main path. Draw the layout on graph paper before moving heavy furniture.
