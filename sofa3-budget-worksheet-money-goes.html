3-Seater Sofa Budget Worksheet: Where the Money Goes

Sofa prices vary widely, and it helps to know which parts of a three-seater you are paying for. This worksheet helps you allocate a budget across what matters most to your household. Use it alongside your own measurements and household routines rather than as a fixed rulebook.

At-a-glance chart

ComponentYour priority (High / Medium / Low)Questions to ask
FrameMaterial and warranty
Seat foam and baseFoam specification and base type
Fabric or leatherAbrasion rating and cleaning code
Delivery and setupFloor charges, lift, assembly
AccessoriesCushions, throws, covers

Practical checks

  • Rank the components before visiting stores.
  • Ask what is included in the quoted price.
  • Confirm delivery and installation charges.
  • Keep a buffer for side tables or a rug.

Making the numbers work at home

Ranking before shopping

Deciding in advance that frame and seat comfort matter more than a fabric trend, for example, keeps you focused when comparing quotes.

Hidden costs

Delivery to upper floors, lift booking and removal of an old sofa can add to the total. Ask for an itemised quote.

A note on adapting this

Treat the suggestions here as a starting framework rather than fixed rules. Fabrics, finishes and household routines differ, so the care label and the seller's instructions always take priority over general advice.

A family replacing a sofa after ten years might rank frame and seat comfort high, fabric medium and accessories low.

Every household is different, so adapt these suggestions rather than following them rigidly. What suits a couple in a studio may not suit a joint family, and the person who uses the sofa most should have the loudest say. Store the care label photo, invoice and your notes together; they will be useful long after delivery day.

Mistakes that are easy to make

  • Signing the delivery note before inspecting the sofa.
  • Discarding packaging before checking every part.
  • Not photographing issues while the delivery team is present.

Step-by-step approach

Compare shortlisted sofas line by line on the same sheet, so differences in size, materials and terms are easy to see. Keep your measurements, swatch notes and photographs in one folder on your phone so the decision stays easy to check later.

Bringing it together
