Moving House in Delhi NCR: Furniture Disassembly and Packing Checklist

Shifting flats within Delhi NCR often means narrow stairwells, society lift bookings and a loading window that the building guard enforces strictly. Most furniture damage happens during packing and carrying rather than on the road. This checklist helps you plan which pieces to dismantle, how to protect them and what to check on arrival.

Moving-day checklist

  • Book the service lift and loading slot with both societies in advance.
  • Photograph every piece, including existing marks, before packing.
  • Put screws and bolts in labelled zip bags taped to the matching part.
  • Measure doors and stair turns at the new flat, not only the old one.
  • Keep a small toolkit (Allen keys, screwdrivers) in the car, not the truck.
  • Unpack and inspect large items before the movers leave.

Weather and timing

During the monsoon, ask for waterproof covers on the truck and avoid leaving upholstered items in open parking areas. In peak summer, early morning moves reduce heat stress on both people and finishes.

Common mistakes

  • Wrapping polished wood directly in stretch film, which can trap moisture.
  • Carrying glass tops flat instead of on edge.
  • Losing the one special bolt that holds the bed together.
