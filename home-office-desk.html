Setup checklist

  • Keep the top near elbow height when seated and position the monitor's top edge near eye level.
  • Allow at least 90 cm behind the chair where someone must walk past.
  • Provide a grommet or rear cable channel without blocking the wall socket.
  • Use a chair mat on delicate flooring and keep the desk away from direct afternoon sun.
  • Measure the doorway and lift before choosing a solid one-piece desk.
