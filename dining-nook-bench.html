Site checklist

  • Keep 75–90 cm behind the bench or chairs for people to move safely.
  • Allow enough table overhang for knees and check skirting-board depth.
  • Choose lift-up storage only if the lid can open without hitting the table.
  • Use washable upholstery or a wipeable finish in a family dining area.
  • Confirm that the bench can pass through the entrance and lift before ordering.
