Corner-sofa checklist

  • Mark the proposed footprint with masking tape for a full day.
  • Keep windows, balcony doors, switches, and AC airflow clear.
  • Choose a reversible chaise if the layout may change.
  • Confirm fabric cleaning for Delhi dust and delivery packaging dimensions.
  • Check assembly, warranty, and the route from gate to room.
