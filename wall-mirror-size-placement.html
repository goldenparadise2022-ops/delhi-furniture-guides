Wall Mirror Size and Placement Guide for Indian Homes

A well-placed mirror makes a compact Delhi flat feel brighter and larger, and it is useful for a last check before leaving home. The right size depends on the furniture below it, the wall height and what the mirror will reflect. These are planning guidelines; follow your family's preferences on placement.

Placement checklist

  • Check what the mirror reflects from the seating area: aim for a window or plant, not clutter.
  • Avoid direct afternoon sun hitting the glass, which can create glare.
  • Use wall plugs and hooks rated for the mirror's weight; ask for help on hollow walls.
  • Choose rounded corners or a frame in homes with small children.
  • Keep mirrors away from damp bathroom-adjacent walls unless rated for humidity.

Frame styles

Wooden frames warm up an entrance, thin metal frames suit modern flats, and frameless bevelled mirrors keep small rooms light. Match one finish already in the room, such as door handles or the console legs.

Common mistakes

  • Hanging a mirror too high for the family's average height.
  • A tiny mirror above a long console.
  • Placing a mirror opposite the TV, doubling screen glare.
