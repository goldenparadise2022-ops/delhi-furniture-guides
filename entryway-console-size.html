Entryway Console Table Size Guide for Delhi Homes

A console should organise keys and parcels without narrowing the entrance. Measure the door swing, corridor and shoe-storage zone first.

Before ordering checklist

  • Measure the clear wall length after door, meter box and switchboard positions.
  • Keep 10–15 cm from a corner so drawers and skirting are usable.
  • Choose rounded corners if children or visitors pass close by.
  • Check lift, stair and doorway measurements for a one-piece top.
  • Use wall anchoring for tall, shallow consoles and keep the top clutter-light.
