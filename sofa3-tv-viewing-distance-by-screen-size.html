3-Seater Sofa Facing the TV: Viewing Distance by Screen Size

Most three-seaters in Indian homes face a television. The distance between sofa and screen affects comfort as much as the seat cushions do, so it is worth planning both together. Keep a pen handy: the table and checklist are meant to be filled in and compared, not just read.

Checklist

  • Measure from the seated eye position, not the sofa front.
  • Subtract sofa depth from room length.
  • Check the TV centre height from the floor.
  • Note glare from windows behind the sofa.

What the table does not show

Eye position, not furniture edge

Viewing distance is measured from where your eyes are when you lean back. On a deep sofa that point can be well behind the front edge, which effectively moves the screen further away.

Screen height

A comfortable TV centre height often sits near seated eye level. Low sofas pair better with lower TV units.

Keeping it practical

Every household is different, so adapt these suggestions rather than following them rigidly. What suits a couple in a studio may not suit a joint family, and the person who uses the sofa most should have the loudest say.

A family with a 55 inch screen in a 10 ft wide room may prefer a 90 cm deep sofa so the seated distance stays within a comfortable range.

Delhi homes vary widely, from compact DDA flats and builder floors to large NCR apartments. Measure the clear floor rather than trusting brochure areas, and note doors, switchboards, windows and AC units before you shortlist anything. Keep your measurements, swatch notes and photographs in one folder on your phone so the decision stays easy to check later.

Pitfalls worth noting

  • Using overall dimensions when the seat dimensions matter more.
  • Placing the sofa first and squeezing everything else around it.
  • Leaving no space for a side table, lamp or charging point.

A simple sequence

Photograph the room from the doorway, mark the planned sofa in tape and photograph it again. Seeing the before and after side by side often makes the right size obvious. A short written record of what you chose and why makes future cleaning, repairs or replacements much simpler.

Before you order

These ranges are practical guidelines rather than rules; sit at the planned distance and watch something for twenty minutes before fixing the layout.
