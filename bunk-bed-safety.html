Children's Bunk Bed Safety Checklist for India

A bunk bed needs safe rails, strong fixing and enough ceiling clearance. Use this check before purchase and again after assembly.

Safety measurements

ItemPractical checkWhy it matters
Top guardrailContinuous on the open side; secure at every jointReduces falls
Mattress fitNo visible gap between mattress and framePrevents entrapment
LadderFirm steps and two-hand gripSafer climbing
CeilingSitting clearance above the top mattressAvoids head strikes

Assembly checklist

  • Confirm the maker's recommended mattress thickness and weight limit.
  • Tighten bolts after one week, then inspect them every few months.
  • Place the ladder away from a door swing, fan and window.
  • Use a wall anchor when the design calls for it; never modify guardrails.
  • Keep cords, stools and sharp furniture away from the climbing side.
