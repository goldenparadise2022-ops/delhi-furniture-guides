Leg Height and Floor Clearance for 3-Seater Sofas: A Cleaning Access Chart

In dusty Delhi homes, the gap beneath a sofa decides whether cleaning is a quick sweep or a monthly struggle. Leg height also affects how light or heavy the sofa looks in a small room. The notes are written with Delhi and NCR homes in mind, though the principles apply to most Indian households.

Practical checks

  • Measure the height of your robot vacuum or mop head.
  • Check whether legs are screw-in and replaceable.
  • Use felt pads suited to marble or tile floors.
  • Plan a monthly move-and-clean under the sofa.

Making the numbers work at home

Robot vacuum compatibility

If you use a robot vacuum, measure its height including any top sensor, then add a small margin. Ask for the exact under-frame clearance rather than leg height alone, because the frame rail may hang lower.

Floor protection

Stone and vitrified tile floors can be scratched by bare legs. Felt or rubber pads protect the floor and make it easier to pull the sofa out for cleaning.

A note on measuring

Treat every figure here as a planning target rather than a promise. Cushion softness, arm shape, rug thickness and household habits all change how a sofa feels and fits, so measure your own room and furniture and leave a little tolerance.

A household cleaning daily with a pocha may choose around 10 cm clearance so the mop reaches the wall without moving the sofa.

Every household is different, so adapt these suggestions rather than following them rigidly. What suits a couple in a studio may not suit a joint family, and the person who uses the sofa most should have the loudest say. Store the care label photo, invoice and your notes together; they will be useful long after delivery day.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Copying a layout from a photo taken in a much larger room.
  • Blocking a window, balcony door or cupboard that is used daily.
  • Skipping the tape-on-the-floor test because the numbers 'look fine'.

Suggested order of work

Start with a rough sketch, add the fixed elements such as doors and windows, then pencil in the sofa. Tape the outline on the floor, walk every route with something in your hands and adjust before you shortlist models. Keep your measurements, swatch notes and photographs in one folder on your phone so the decision stays easy to check later.
