Sofa-cum-Bed Size and Mechanism Guide for Delhi Guest Rooms

In many Delhi flats the study or second living area doubles as a guest room for a few nights a year. A sofa-cum-bed can cover both roles, but only if the open bed fits the room and the mechanism suits the people who will operate it. Treat the figures below as planning targets and confirm exact sizes with the seller.

Before you order

  • Measure the room with the bed open, not closed, and leave about 60 cm on at least one side to walk.
  • Check that the open bed does not block a wardrobe door, balcony door or AC drain pipe.
  • Ask for the mattress thickness and whether a separate topper will fit when folded.
  • Operate the mechanism yourself in the showroom at least three times.
  • Confirm storage: some models hide a bedding box under the seat.

Daily sofa, occasional bed

Most sofa-cum-beds spend 95% of the year as sofas, so judge seat comfort first. A very soft seat that feels pleasant for guests can feel tiring for daily television or work calls. Firm, flat seating usually makes a better bed as well.

Common mistakes

  • Buying a model that opens towards the only door.
  • Forgetting that bedding needs a storage place when the guest leaves.
  • Choosing a heavy mechanism that the household's elders cannot open alone.

For example, a family in a Laxmi Nagar 2BHK might place a pull-out model along the study wall so the open bed runs parallel to the window, keeping the door swing clear.
