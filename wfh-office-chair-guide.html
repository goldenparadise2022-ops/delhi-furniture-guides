Work-From-Home Office Chair Guide for Delhi Flats

A desk can be improvised, but a poor chair makes every working hour harder. In compact Delhi flats the chair also has to fit a small bedroom corner and roll without damaging tiles. This guide covers adjustments, sizes and materials to compare. It is general comfort guidance; consult a healthcare professional for persistent pain.

Before you buy

  • Measure your desk height and the space behind it: allow about 90 cm to push back.
  • Use a chair mat or soft castors on tiles and wood flooring.
  • Check the base diameter fits between bed and desk.
  • Sit for at least ten minutes in the showroom with adjustments set.
  • Ask about warranty on the gas lift and mechanism.

Common mistakes

  • Choosing a gaming-style chair too wide for a bedroom corner.
  • Setting seat height for the chair instead of for your desk.
  • Keeping armrests that stop the chair sliding under the desk.
