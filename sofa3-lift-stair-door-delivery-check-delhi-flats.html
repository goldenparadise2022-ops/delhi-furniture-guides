Lift, Stair and Door Check for Delivering a 3-Seater Sofa in Delhi Flats

Many sofa returns begin at the building entrance rather than in the living room. Delhi housing mixes old walk-ups, builder floors with tight stairwells and high-rises with small service lifts, so the delivery route deserves its own measurement sheet. Keep a pen handy: the table and checklist are meant to be filled in and compared, not just read.

At-a-glance chart

Route pointWhat to measureCommon problem
Building gate and lobbyClear width and any stepSecurity barriers or planters
Lift carInternal width, depth, height and diagonalSofa too long to stand upright
Stair landingTurning space at each landingLow beams on mid-landings
Flat doorClear opening with the door fully openSafety grille reducing the width
Internal corridorWidth at the living room turnWardrobe or shoe rack in the path

Practical checks

  • Photograph each point with the tape measure visible.
  • Share the measurements with the seller before confirming.
  • Ask whether the legs are detachable.
  • Book the service lift with your society in advance.

Making the numbers work at home

The diagonal test

A sofa that will not pass flat can sometimes be tilted on end. Measure the diagonal of the doorway and the height of the sofa back to see whether tilting helps.

Builder-floor stairwells

Narrow stairs with a U-turn are often the tightest point. Measure the landing depth and ceiling height at the turn, because the sofa must pivot there.

Keeping it practical

Every household is different, so adapt these suggestions rather than following them rigidly. What suits a couple in a studio may not suit a joint family, and the person who uses the sofa most should have the loudest say.

In a fourth-floor walk-up in Lajpat Nagar, the iron grille at the flat door may be narrower than the door itself; removing two bolts on delivery day can save a return.

Delhi's seasons put different demands on furniture: dry, dusty heat, humid monsoon weeks and cool winters with heaters. Revisit your routine as the seasons change rather than setting it once. Store the care label photo, invoice and your notes together; they will be useful long after delivery day.

Pitfalls worth noting

  • Confirming the order before checking the delivery route.
  • Relying on verbal answers instead of written confirmation.
  • Leaving delivery charges and floor access to the last minute.

A simple sequence

Gather dimensions, route photos and policy details in one note, check each listing against it, and keep screenshots of whatever you finally order. Keep your measurements, swatch notes and photographs in one folder on your phone so the decision stays easy to check later.

Bringing it together

Send the photographed route to the seller along with the sofa dimensions, and ask for confirmation in writing.
