Furniture Budget Planner for a New 2BHK in Delhi NCR: Room-by-Room Priorities

Moving into a new 2BHK usually means buying many pieces at once, and the budget can disappear before the essentials are covered. Splitting spending by room and priority keeps the flat liveable from day one and leaves room to add pieces later. The percentages below are an example split, not a rule; adjust them for your household.

Example budget split

RoomShare of budget (example)Buy firstCan wait
Master bedroom25–30%Bed, mattress, wardrobeDresser, bench
Living room25–30%Sofa, TV unitAccent chair, rug
Dining15–20%Table and chairsCrockery unit
Second bedroom / study15–20%Bed or deskBookshelf
Entry and balcony5–10%Shoe storageBalcony seating

Priority checklist

  • Spend most on what you use every day: mattress, sofa and dining chairs.
  • Measure every room before shopping and note doors, windows and switchboards.
  • Keep 5–10% aside for delivery, installation and small fixes.
  • Ask for itemised quotes that include floor charges and assembly.
  • Buy storage early; clutter makes a new flat feel small quickly.

Phasing purchases

Phase 1 covers sleeping, sitting, eating and storing clothes. Phase 2, after a month of living in the flat, adds pieces you now know you need, such as a console where parcels pile up or a bookshelf where papers collect. Living in the space first prevents expensive guesses.

Common mistakes

  • Spending heavily on decor before buying a good mattress.
  • Ordering everything online without checking lift and stair access.
  • Forgetting curtains and lighting in the budget.

A couple moving into a Greater Noida West 2BHK, for example, might buy the bed, sofa and dining set in the first week and add the study desk after a month.
