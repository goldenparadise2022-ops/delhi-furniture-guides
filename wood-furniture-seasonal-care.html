Wooden Furniture Care Through Delhi's Seasons: A Month-by-Month Guide

Wood responds to the air around it. Delhi's year swings from dusty, dry heat to sticky monsoon humidity and then to heater-dried winter air, so a single care routine rarely works all year. This seasonal guide keeps beds, tables and cabinets looking good with a few minutes of effort each week. Always follow the finish instructions from the maker first.

Seasonal care calendar

SeasonMain riskWhat to do
March–June (hot, dusty)Dust scratches, sun fading, dryingDust with a soft dry cloth; draw curtains in the afternoon
July–September (monsoon)Swelling, sticky drawers, fungusKeep a gap from damp walls; air cupboards on dry days
October–November (festive)Heat from diyas, heavy useUse trays and mats; clean spills promptly
December–February (winter)Heater dryness, cracksKeep heaters at least 1 m away; avoid direct hot air

Weekly checklist

  • Dust with a microfibre cloth before wiping so grit does not scratch.
  • Wipe spills straight away with a barely damp cloth, then dry.
  • Use coasters, placemats and felt pads under decor.
  • Open drawers and cupboards briefly on dry monsoon days.
  • Check for loose handles and joints once a month.

What to avoid

  • Wet mopping close to furniture legs.
  • Silicone sprays not approved by the maker; they can build up on some finishes.
  • Placing furniture in direct sun or beside an AC outdoor-unit opening.

When to call a professional

White rings that do not fade, cracks that widen over a season, or drawers that stay stuck after the monsoon usually need a carpenter or polisher. Early repair is simpler than a full refinish later.

A family in a Rohini builder floor, for example, might move a sheesham chest a few centimetres from the external wall each July and back in October.
